******************
Activity data
******************

Your activity data describes your development and humanitarian work — referred to as "activities" in IATI. It includes details such as where your activities take place, how they're financed, and what results they achieve.

The table below lists the information you can include about an activity, and shows whether each item is mandatory, recommended, or optional.

.. tip::

   For the formal specification, please refer to the `IATI Standard activity reference <https://iatistandard.org/en/iati-standard/203/activity-standard/>`_.
